Where to find your serial number

Hardware serial numbers start with SA and are eleven characters long. They sit in a different place depending on what you have.

  • bizhub multifunctionInside the front door, on a white label near the toner bay. Also printed at the top of any meter reading page.
  • bizhub PRESS and PROOn the left side panel near the power switch, or under Machine Information on the control panel.
  • Desktop printersOn the rear of the unit, below the power socket.
  • Production and finishingEach module carries its own label. Use the serial on the main engine.
  • Software licencesOn your licence certificate or order confirmation email. These do not start with SA.
